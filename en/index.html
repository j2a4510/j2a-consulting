From Lomé, J2A Consulting connects project owners, local governments and investors. We structure projects, raise the financing and support implementation, from West Africa to the Gulf, Quebec and Asia.

Discuss your project Our approach

Between a good project and its financing, what is often missing is a file.

Many sound projects never get financed. Money is not the problem: what is missing is a file that meets bank and fund standards, a clear legal structure, a phased plan and the right partners in the right place.

That missing link is what we build. We work for companies, for foreign investors entering the market, and for local governments that need to turn their needs into financeable projects.

  1. 1

    Study

    Market, feasibility, local partners. We validate the project before committing resources.

  2. 2

    Structure

    Legal and financial set-up, phasing, financial model, governance.

  3. 3

    Finance

    Bank- and investor-grade files, sourcing and negotiation with financiers.

  4. 4

    Implement

    On-the-ground execution, partners, local representation.
