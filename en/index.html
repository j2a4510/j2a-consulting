A good project is not enough. Between the idea and the financing, what is missing is almost always a file.

A file that meets bank and fund standards, a clear legal structure, a phased plan, the right partners in the right place: that is what separates a sound project from a financed one.

That missing link is what we build, for companies, for foreign investors entering the market, and for local governments that need to turn their needs into financeable projects.

A project's journey

Four steps, one partner, from the first diagnosis to operations on the ground.

1

Study

We validate the project before committing resources.

  • Market and feasibility study
  • Local partner identification
  • Go / no-go diagnosis
2

Structure

We shape the project the way financiers expect it.

  • Legal set-up and governance
  • Phasing and financial model
  • Buyer contracts
3

Finance

We source the financing and negotiate it.

  • Bank and investor files
  • Local banks, funds, donors
  • Negotiation and closing
4

Implement

We stay with the project on the ground.

  • Execution and monitoring
  • Hiring and partners
  • Local representation
