A good project is not enough. Between the idea and the financing, what is missing is almost always a file.

A file that meets bank and fund standards, a clear legal structure, a phased plan, the right partners in the right place: that is what separates a sound project from a financed one.

That missing link is what we build, for companies, for foreign investors entering the market, and for local governments that need to turn their needs into financeable projects.

Cooperatives and agricultural value chains

Cooperatives ready to sell and to be financed

Cooperatives and village associations have the land and the know-how. What they usually lack is a buyer under contract, a recognised organisation and access to finance. We make the link, from the first diagnosis to monitoring each season.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis and rating

    Legal status, land, governance, volumes, accounts. Each cooperative gets an A, B or C rating: what can be financed today, and what still needs to be built.

  2. 02

    Structuring

    Compliance with the OHADA Uniform Act on cooperatives, governance, simple bookkeeping and manager training.

  3. 03

    Certification and internal control

    Preparation for group organic certification and running the internal control system that certification bodies require.

  4. 04

    Market access

    Finding buyers and drafting purchase contracts: volumes, quality, floor prices and premiums.

  5. 05

    Seasonal financing

    Crop finance backed by purchase contracts, warehouse receipts, guarantees and grants. Funds go directly to the cooperatives.

  6. 06

    Reporting to financiers

    Regular reports on deliveries, stocks and repayments, so everyone knows where the season stands.
